Definitions
Hourly billing value
Σ billable hours × the billing rate snapshotted on each entry for its work date, for hourly projects only. Rate changes don't alter past entries unless an administrator recalculates.
Estimated labour cost
Σ hours × each person's private cost rate for the work date. Excludes overhead, taxes and expenses.
Fixed-fee comparison
The agreed fee minus estimated labour cost over the whole project: an estimate of labour-only contribution, never an amount owed.
