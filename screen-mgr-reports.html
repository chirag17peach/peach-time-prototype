How these numbers are calculated
Total hours
Sum of finalized entry durations (exact seconds) ÷ 3600. Running timers are excluded until stopped.
Billable share
Billable hours ÷ all logged hours × 100. N/A when nothing is logged.
Hourly billing value
For billable entries on hourly projects: hours × the rate snapshotted on each entry for its work date. Entries without a rate are excluded and listed. This is not revenue or an invoice.
