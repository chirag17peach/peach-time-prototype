Definitions (please read before comparing people)
Available
Scheduled working hours from each person's schedule, inside their employment dates, minus organization holidays and recorded leave, counted up to today.
Missing
For each week, scheduled hours minus logged hours when positive. A reminder to check timesheets, never a submission requirement.
Billable utilization
Billable hours ÷ available hours × 100. N/A when nothing was scheduled. Non-billable work (internal projects, admin, training) is real work. These figures describe where time was recorded, not how well anyone performed.
